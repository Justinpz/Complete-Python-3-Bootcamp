# ReRack — Visual Guidelines (Image Grammar)

> **QUICK REFERENCE**
> - **Light:** Hard, directional, single source. Deep shadows, high contrast, no blown highlights.
> - **Grade:** Matte, desaturated, warm tungsten midtones, filmic grain.
> - **Composition:** Structured, geometric, negative space, centered or rule-of-thirds. Stillness over chaos.
> - **Aspect:** 4:5 (1080×1350) feed · 9:16 (1080×1920) Reels/Stories · 1:1 only for grid-sync exceptions.
> - **Mood:** Composed, serious, unapproachable. Never smiling, never busy, never bright.

---

## 1. Lighting

- **One hard directional source.** Golden-hour sun, a single gym fixture, or tungsten practical. Sculpt with shadow.
- **Deep shadows, retained detail.** Crushed blacks that still read texture. High contrast.
- **No blown highlights.** Protect highlight roll-off — matte, never glossy.
- **Time of day:** pre-dawn, blue-hour, late-golden, or windowless interior. Avoid flat midday and soft overcast.
- **Practicals welcome:** exposed tungsten, work lamps, fluorescent bays — used sparingly and on-palette.

## 2. Color Grade

- Pull saturation down 10–20%. Monochrome-leaning.
- Warm midtones (tungsten), cool-neutral shadows.
- Lift deepest black ~3–5% for matte filmic base.
- Fine, consistent film grain on every asset.
- Stay inside the approved palette (see `color-palette.md`).

## 3. Composition

- **Geometry first.** Use architectural lines — rack uprights, doorframes, concrete seams, horizon — as structure.
- **Negative space is mandatory** on The Standard (≥60% empty for text). Generous on all pillars.
- **Centered or thirds.** Subject dead-center for authority, or on a third for tension. No casual snapshots.
- **Stillness.** Even action shots should feel composed and intentional — a held moment, not a blur.
- **One subject.** Never crowd the frame. The model is alone; the object is isolated.
- **Eye-line / camera height:** chest-to-eye level. Avoid playful low/Dutch angles except deliberate Drop tension.

## 4. Environment

Approved worlds only:
- Industrial gym (steel racks, rubber floor, chalk, concrete).
- Raw architecture (concrete, exposed structure, corridors, stairwells).
- Minimal seamless (black void, charcoal, bone/cream field).
- Exterior structure at blue/golden hour (loading docks, lots, brutalist forms).

Never: bright commercial gyms, homes, nature/scenic, nightlife, crowds, colorful sets.

## 5. Aspect Ratios

| Use | Ratio | Pixels |
|---|---|---|
| Feed (default) | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 |
| Reels / Stories | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Grid-sync / carousel exception | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 |

Default to 4:5 for all static feed posts. Shoot/generate Reels and Stories natively at 9:16 — never crop a 4:5 up.

## 6. Per-Pillar Visual Notes

| Pillar | Lighting | Composition | Texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Standard | Minimal, often a single shaft of light on concrete | Massive negative space, text zone reserved | Concrete, void |
| The Uniform | Hard hero light, sculpted shadow | Centered or thirds, full/half body | Fabric drape, skin, steel |
| The Work | Moody practicals, breath/chalk in light | Tighter, kinetic-but-composed | Sweat, chalk, knurling, rubber |
| The Object | Raking side light for texture | Macro, isolated, shallow depth | Weave, stitch, woven label, garment-dye mottle |
| The Drop | High contrast, hard rim, urgency | Centered, countdown/label space | Product + type lockup |

## 7. The Consistency Test

Before any asset ships, it must pass all five:
1. Could this sit beside the last 9 posts without breaking the grid? (Y)
2. Is it inside the approved palette and grade? (Y)
3. Is there one subject, hard light, real negative space? (N to clutter)
4. Does it avoid every "forbidden" cue (neon, smiling, cringe, logos)? (Y)
5. Does it feel composed and serious, not snapshot or hype? (Y)

## 8. Forbidden Visual Cues

❌ Smiling / casual / candid energy · ❌ Bright commercial gym · ❌ Neon, gels, saturated color · ❌ Crowds or multiple people · ❌ Visible non-ReRack logos · ❌ Cheesy military props (flags, eagles, camo, dog tags) · ❌ Lens flares, light leaks, HDR look · ❌ Motivational stock-photo energy · ❌ Cluttered backgrounds · ❌ Blown-out white studio.
