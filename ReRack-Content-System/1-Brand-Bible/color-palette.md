# ReRack — Color Palette

> **QUICK REFERENCE**
> - **Core neutrals:** Black `#0A0A0A` · White `#F5F4F0` · Bone `#E8E2D5` · Cream `#EDE7D8`
> - **Tactical earth:** Tactical Tan `#B7A98B` · Olive Drab `#5A5C45` · Washed Charcoal `#3A3A3C` · Steel Gray `#8A8D8F`
> - **Rule:** Monochrome-first. One earth accent maximum per image. Never neon, never saturated.

---

## 1. Approved Palette

These are the **only** colors permitted in ReRack content, product, and graphics. Garments, backdrops, grade, and typography all draw from this set.

## 2. Usage Rules

- **Monochrome-first.** Default to black/white/charcoal/steel. Earth tones (tan, olive, bone, cream) are accents and colorways, not floods.
- **One accent per image.** A single olive or tan note is powerful. Two competing earth tones reads cheap.
- **Warm, never clinical.** Use `#F5F4F0` warm white, not pure white. Our world is tungsten and concrete, not a lightbox.
- **Backdrops:** concrete (steel gray), black void, washed charcoal seamless, or bone/cream warm field. Never colored gels.
- **Type:** White on dark, or black on bone/cream. Never colored type except a rare olive/tan for `The Drop` accents.
- **Garment hero:** let the colorway be the color story. The environment supports, never competes.

## 3. Grade / Color Treatment

- High contrast, crushed-but-detailed shadows.
- Slightly desaturated overall; pull saturation **down** 10–20%.
- Warm midtones (tungsten bias), cool-neutral shadows.
- Matte finish — lift the deepest blacks ~3–5% for a filmic, non-digital feel.
- Subtle film grain on every asset.

## 4. Forbidden Colors

❌ Neon anything · ❌ Saturated primary red/blue/green · ❌ Pure white `#FFFFFF` · ❌ Pastels · ❌ Gold/metallic gradients · ❌ Patriotic red-white-blue combos · ❌ Gym-brand electric accents (volt, cyan, magenta).

If a color isn't in Section 1, it doesn't ship.
