# ReRack — Voice & Tone

> **QUICK REFERENCE**
> - **Tone:** Direct, disciplined, intelligent, serious, concise, emotionally controlled.
> - **Caption formula:** Line 1 = hard truth (3–6 words) · Line 2 = expansion/tension · Line 3 = brand stamp (`FORGED DAILY.` or product reference).
> - **Reel hook formula:** one line, 5–12 words, high tension, no punctuation.
> - **Never:** cheesy military, patriotism, cringe alpha, hustle quotes, emojis, exclamation points, hashtag spam.

---

## 1. The Voice

ReRack speaks like a disciplined man who has nothing to prove. Short sentences. No filler. No hype. Intelligence implied, never performed. The voice is **calm authority** — it states the standard and lets the reader measure themselves against it.

Think: a quiet mentor, a former operator, a man who has rebuilt himself and refuses to dramatize it.

## 2. Tone Dials

| Dial | Setting |
|---|---|
| Formality | Plainspoken, not corporate, not slang |
| Energy | Controlled. Low, steady heat — never shouting |
| Emotion | Felt but governed. We acknowledge the cost; we don't wallow |
| Certainty | High. We assert. We do not hedge or ask permission |
| Length | As short as possible. Every word earns its place |

## 3. Caption Framework (the formula)

```
Line 1:  A standard or hard truth        (3–6 words)
Line 2:  Expansion, tension, personal truth
Line 3:  Brand stamp                     (FORGED DAILY. or product reference)
```

**Example**
> You don't rise to your goals. You fall to your standards.
>
> The gym doesn't care how you feel. Show up anyway.
>
> FORGED DAILY.

**Variations allowed:**
- 2-line minimal (hard truth + stamp).
- 4-line extended (truth + two-beat expansion + stamp) for The Standard anchors.
- Product-reference close instead of tagline for The Uniform/Object/Drop.

## 4. Reel Hook Framework

```
One line. 5–12 words. High tension. No punctuation.
Pair with a 7–15s cinematic clip.
```

**Examples**
> Discipline over emotion every single day
> Nobody is coming to save you so train
> The standard does not move for you

## 5. Approved Messaging Patterns

- **The standard statement:** "You don't rise to your goals. You fall to your standards."
- **The cost acknowledgment:** "Rebuilding hurts. That's how you know it's real."
- **The responsibility line:** "Nobody is coming. That's the good news."
- **The contrast (motivation vs discipline):** "Motivation is a guest. Discipline lives here."
- **The uniform reference:** "Heavyweight. Garment-dyed. Built to be worn into the ground."
- **The drop / scarcity:** "Cycle 001. Ten pieces. When it's gone, it's gone."

## 6. Forbidden Phrases & Moves

❌ Hustle/grind clichés ("rise and grind," "no days off," "team no sleep")
❌ Cringe alpha-male language ("sigma," "high value male," "stay hard" overuse)
❌ Patriotic/military cosplay ("freedom," "warrior," "sheepdog," flags)
❌ Hype-influencer energy ("link in bio fam," "drop a 🔥," "who's with me")
❌ Exclamation points · Emojis in captions · Hashtag walls (max 3 quiet tags, lowercase)
❌ Questions that beg engagement ("agree? 👇")
❌ Self-help platitudes ("manifest," "good vibes," "trust the process" as filler)
❌ Apologizing, hedging, over-explaining the product

## 7. Mechanics

- **Punctuation:** periods and the occasional em dash. No exclamation points. No ellipses for drama.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case in body; `FORGED DAILY.` and SKU labels in caps.
- **Hashtags (traction policy):** 3 brand tags (`#forgeddaily #rerack #cycle001`) **+ 2 rotating high-traffic/trending tags** relevant to discipline / men's fitness / tactical-minimal apparel = **5 total**, lowercase, own line. Rotate the 2 trending tags every post (never the same block twice in a row). Best placed in the **first comment** to keep the caption clean.
- **Emojis:** none. Ever.
- **CTAs:** rare and flat. "Cycle 001. Link in bio." Not "RUN don't walk!!"

## 8. The Cross-Reference / "Pull" Mechanism

Captions can build a thread across days. A Monday Standard post can plant a phrase ("Fall to your standards.") that a Thursday post answers ("Today the standard held."). Use the `caption-framework.md` pull-tags to chain a week into a small narrative arc — rewarding the follower who reads every day.

## 9. The Voice Test

Before any caption ships:
1. Could a hype influencer have written this? If yes → rewrite.
2. Is there a single emoji, exclamation point, or cliché? → remove.
3. Is it shorter than it could be? → cut more.
4. Does it state a standard rather than beg for engagement? → required.
5. Does it sound like a man with nothing to prove? → ship.
