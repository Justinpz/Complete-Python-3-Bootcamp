## ReRack Archetype — ATLAS (`ATLAS_RR_001`)

ATLAS is not a model in the fashion sense. He is the embodied standard — the man the brand says you could become. He is composed, earned, unhurried, and unbothered. He never performs for the camera; the camera observes him.

### Physical Specification
- **Age:** 28–32 (reads early 30s — past striving, into mastery).
- **Build:** Lean, disciplined, athletic. Visible conditioning without bodybuilder bulk. Functional strength, not aesthetics-for-aesthetics.
- **Height:** ~6'0" / 183cm (tall enough to carry the oversized silhouette).
- **Ethnicity:** Ambiguous-Mediterranean / mixed — warm olive skin tone that holds in tungsten light. Broadly aspirational, not narrowly cast. *(Lock the exact reference once training photos are selected; keep it identical forever after.)*
- **Facial hair:** Short, deliberate stubble-to-short-beard. Groomed but not fussy. Never clean-shaven, never wild.
- **Hair:** Dark brown/black, short, structured. Textured crop or short slick. Never long, never styled-soft.

### Expression & Demeanor
- **Resting state:** Composed, controlled, slightly intense.
- **Emotion range:** Focused · determined · calm · unapproachable. (Never: cheerful, goofy, surprised, flirtatious.)
- **Eye contact:** Direct and confident, or off-camera/middle-distance. **Never** a wide smile. A faint, earned half-expression at most.
- **Posture:** Upright, structural, disciplined. He occupies space without sprawling.

### Wardrobe Constants
- **Primary:** Black ReRack heavyweight oversized tee (drop shoulder, boxy). Rotate to Bone / Washed Charcoal / Olive Drab / Tactical Tan only as colorway features.
- **Bottoms:** Tapered black or charcoal trousers/joggers, or muted shorts for Work shots. Always on-palette.
- **Accessories:** Minimal — chalk, a simple matte watch, plain wrist wraps. No jewelry, no chains, no rings.
- **Footwear:** Muted — gray, black, bone, white. No loud sneakers.
- **Never:** visible non-ReRack logos, bright colors, patterns, layered "fit" styling.

### Pose Library (signature recurring angles)
1. **Standing frontal** — arms at sides, weight even, looking into lens. The authority shot.
2. **3/4 turn, structural lean** — shoulder against a rack upright / concrete wall, gaze off-camera.
3. **Arms crossed, centered, contemplative** — chest-up, slight downward eye-line.
4. **Mid-motion** — set-up, lift, chalk clap, walk-out. Composed, not blurred.
5. **Seated, side angle** — on a bench, forearms on knees, recovering between sets.

### Lighting Preference
- Hard directional light (golden hour, single gym fixture, tungsten practical).
- Deep shadows, high contrast, no blown highlights.
- Film grain, matte finish. Warm midtones.

### Context Rules
- Always in industrial / gym / minimal environments.
- **Never** smiling, never casual, never goofing.
- **Never** with other people — ATLAS is always alone.
- **Never** in branded/logo clothing except ReRack.
