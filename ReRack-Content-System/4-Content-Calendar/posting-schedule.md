# ReRack — Posting Schedule & Instagram Best Practices

**Timing rules and cadence for the ReRack feed.**
**Tagline stamp:** `FORGED DAILY.`

> One disciplined post per day. The Standard anchors; product punctuates. This document defines *when* to post, *which slot* each pillar belongs in, and the weekly rhythm that keeps the feed coherent. All times **EST** (the audience's primary timezone). Maintain a single timezone for consistency — do not chase local peaks.

---

## 1. The three peak slots

ReRack posts into three Instagram engagement peaks. Each carries a different psychological mode, and each pillar is matched to the slot that fits its job.

| Slot | Time (EST) | Mode | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | **6:00 AM** | Intention | The disciplined man checks his phone before training. Philosophy lands as a standard to carry into the day. |
| **Midday** | **3:00 PM** | Browse / shop | Afternoon lull, considered scrolling. Product gets evaluated and saved; shopping intent is highest. |
| **Evening** | **8:00 PM** | Cinematic / urgency | Highest reach window. Best for Reels (cinema, movement) and Drop urgency (launch, low-stock). |

---

## 2. Pillar → slot mapping

| Pillar | Default slot | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **The Standard** (static) | **6:00 AM** | Philosophy as a morning intention. Sets the day's standard. |
| **The Standard** (Reel) | **8:00 PM** | Identity Reels earn cinematic evening reach. |
| **The Uniform** | **3:00 PM** | Product on model — caught in the afternoon shopping mindset. |
| **The Object** | **3:00 PM** | Macro craft detail — drives consideration and saves at browse time. |
| **The Work** (static) | **8:00 PM** | Moody grind scenes hit hardest after dark. |
| **The Work** (Reel) | **8:00 PM** | Movement + tension peak in the evening window. |
| **The Drop** | **8:00 PM** | Launches and scarcity need the biggest, most urgent audience. |

**Rule of thumb:** every Reel posts at **8:00 PM**. The Standard (static) owns **6:00 AM**. Product (Uniform / Object) owns **3:00 PM**.

---

## 3. Weekly cadence template

One post per day, seven days a week. Reels are always The Work or The Standard, never static product.

| Day | Pillar lean | Format | Slot | Reel? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | The Standard (anchor) | Static | 6:00 AM | — |
| **Tue** | The Work | Reel | 8:00 PM | ✅ Reel 1 |
| **Wed** | The Uniform | Static | 3:00 PM | — |
| **Thu** | The Standard | Reel | 8:00 PM | ✅ Reel 2 |
| **Fri** | The Object | Static | 3:00 PM | — |
| **Sat** | The Standard / The Work | Reel | 8:00 PM | ✅ Reel 3 |
| **Sun** | The Uniform / The Work | Static | 3:00 PM / 8:00 PM | — |

- **3 Reels per week**, spread Tue / Thu / Sat so movement never clusters and never goes more than ~2 days without a Reel.
- **Monday always re-anchors** on The Standard — reset the feed after the weekend.
- **The Drop** displaces the lean above during launch beats (tease / launch / sell-through), taking the 8:00 PM slot.
- This is a *template*, not a straitjacket — the live calendar shifts pillars to hit the monthly mix and to honor the no-back-to-back-product rule. The cadence (3 Reels, Monday anchor, product mid-week) is what stays fixed.

> Hard constraint that overrides the template: **never schedule The Uniform and The Object on consecutive days.** If two product posts must fall in one week, separate them with a Standard or Work day (ideally a Reel).

---

## 4. Heavy product push

Run a **Uniform + Object combo every 7–10 days** — two product posts inside one week to spike consideration — but **separated by at least one non-product day** (a Work Reel is ideal). Escalate push frequency as a drop approaches (see `90-day-calendar.md`).

---

## 5. Stories cadence

Stories are the daily texture between grid posts — looser, but still on-brand (same grade, same restraint, no memes).

- **2–4 Stories per day**, most days. Dark days are fine; do not pad to hit a number.
- **Morning (6–9 AM):** a single Standard line or a behind-the-rack training frame — set the day's tone.
- **Afternoon (2–4 PM):** re-share the day's feed post; add a poll or "tap if you train today" interaction.
- **Evening (7–10 PM):** process clips, chalk, breath in light — the raw cut of The Work.
- **Always:** Story-tease every feed post within an hour of it going live (drive grid reach).
- **Drop windows:** countdown stickers, low-stock counters, and a persistent "Cycle 001" Highlight. Stories carry the urgency the grid keeps restrained.
- Keep a permanent **Highlights** set: `STANDARD` · `THE WORK` · `CYCLE 001` · `FORGED`.

---

## 6. Frequency guidance

- **Feed:** exactly **1 post/day** (7/week). Consistency beats volume — the grid is the brand.
- **Reels:** **3/week** (ramping to 4/week in the launch phase). Quality over quantity; a weak Reel dilutes the cinema.
- **Stories:** **daily** texture, 2–4 frames.
- **Carousels:** sparingly, for The Object (detail sequences) or a multi-frame Standard statement. Default to single 4:5 stills.
- **Never** post twice in the same slot on the same day. **Never** skip the Monday anchor.
- Protect the grade: every asset must pass the 5-point Consistency Test before it ships (see `visual-guidelines.md`).

---

## 7. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Post the Monday Standard anchor at 6:00 AM, every week, without fail.
- Keep all three Reels (Tue/Thu/Sat) at 8:00 PM for cinematic reach.
- Put product (Uniform/Object) in the 3:00 PM browse slot.
- Story-tease every feed post within the hour.
- Separate any two product posts with a Standard or Work day.
- Hold one consistent timezone (EST) across the whole calendar.
- Close captions and final Reel frames with `FORGED DAILY.`
- Let The Standard outnumber every other pillar — it anchors the feed.

**Don't**
- Don't post The Uniform and The Object on back-to-back days. Ever.
- Don't make a Reel out of static product — Reels are The Work or The Standard only.
- Don't use exclamation points, emojis, or hype language on the tagline.
- Don't chase trending audio or formats that break the grade.
- Don't post off-peak "just to post" — a dark slot beats a weak post.
- Don't cluster all Reels in one stretch of the week.
- Don't let product lead the feed during the Foundation phase — identity first.
- Don't break the monochrome, hard-light grade for engagement bait.

`FORGED DAILY.`
