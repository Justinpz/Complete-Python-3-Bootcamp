## MONTH 1 — Foundation phase
**Weeks 1–4 · 06/08–07/05**

### Strategic intent
Establish *who ReRack is* before asking anyone to buy. The feed must read as a coherent identity at a glance — composed, serious, monochrome, disciplined. The Standard does the heavy lifting; product appears as proof the world is real, never as a pitch. By the end of Month 1 a cold visitor should be able to scroll nine posts and feel the philosophy without reading a single caption.

### Weekly pillar emphasis
- **Wk 1 — Anchor.** Standard-heavy (≈3 Standard / week). Seed the world: gym-as-cathedral, concrete, single shaft of light. One Uniform and one Object only, to prove product exists.
- **Wk 2 — Ramp the Work.** Hold Standard as the spine; introduce more The Work (process, grind). Product still punctuates, never clusters.
- **Wk 3 — First product push.** Run the month's heavy product push (one Uniform + one Object in the same week, separated by a Work Reel). Late-week, plant the **first Drop tease** for Cycle 001.
- **Wk 4 — Identity over transaction.** Re-anchor on Standard; one early Drop beat to register that ReRack is drop-based, then return to identity.

### Reel cadence
3 Reels / week, all The Work or The Standard. Placement: one mid-week Work Reel (process), two identity Reels (Standard) on the bookends. Evening (8 PM EST) for cinematic reach.

### Key milestones / drop beats
- Wk 1 Day 1: brand-defining anchor post (no product).
- Wk 3: first heavy product push + first Drop tease.
- Wk 4: first explicit "drops, not always-on inventory" beat.
- End of Month 1: grid passes the 9-post Consistency Test.

### KPIs to watch
- **Profile-visit → follow rate** (identity resonance).
- **Saves on The Standard posts** (philosophy landing).
- **Grid coherence** (qualitative: does it pass the Consistency Test?).
- **Follower quality** (engaged men 22–35, not vanity reach).
- Baseline **reach per Reel** to benchmark Months 2–3.

## MONTH 2 — Shadow seeding phase
**Weeks 5–9 · 07/06–08/09**

### Strategic intent
Build recognition for **ATLAS** (the recurring model / face of the Uniform) so the audience starts to recognize a *person*, not just a product — and ramp The Work and The Uniform so the brand feels lived-in and inevitable. Begin teasing Cycle 001 in the shadows: cropped SKUs, woven labels half-seen, dates implied not stated. The goal is anticipation without a hard sell, so that by Month 3 the launch lands on a warm, primed audience.

### Weekly pillar emphasis
- **Wk 5–6 — ATLAS recognition.** Lift The Uniform toward its full 25% share; ATLAS appears consistently in the same hard hero light so the face becomes familiar. Standard still anchors each week.
- **Wk 7 — Work intensifies.** Push The Work to full weight — sweat, chalk, knurling, breath in light. The grind becomes the proof that the upcoming drop is earned.
- **Wk 8 — Object as tease.** Lean The Object into macro teases of Cycle 001 SKUs (embroidery, garment-dye mottle, woven label) — shown, never named. One heavy product push this week.
- **Wk 9 — Bridge to launch.** Begin the Drop ramp: scarcity language, "Cycle 001 is coming," countdown framing seeded. Keep Standard present so it never tips into hype.

### Reel cadence
3–4 Reels / week. Add a fourth Reel in Wk 7 and Wk 9 to carry The Work's intensity and the pre-launch tension. Still exclusively The Work / The Standard; product remains static.

### Key milestones / drop beats
- Wk 5: ATLAS established as the recognizable face of the Uniform.
- Wk 7: peak Work intensity (most kinetic Reels of the quarter so far).
- Wk 8: heavy product push with Cycle 001 macro teases.
- Wk 9: countdown framing begins; email list primed (email = primary channel).

### KPIs to watch
- **ATLAS post recognition** (comments referencing the model / returning engagement on his posts).
- **Email list growth rate** (primary channel — the real launch lever).
- **Reel reach + completion** (does The Work hold attention?).
- **Tease-post saves / shares** (anticipation building).
- **Share-of-Uniform climbing toward 25%** without breaking the no-back-to-back rule.

## MONTH 3 — Cycle 001 launch phase
**Weeks 10–13 · 08/10–09/06**

### Strategic intent
Convert the primed audience. The Drop intensifies to its full 10% share and concentrates into a countdown → launch → sell-through arc. Product (Uniform + Object) carries more of the feed during the live window, but The Standard still steadies the brand so the launch reads as a disciplined release, not a hype sale. The 10-SKU Cycle 001 lineup sells through; the phase closes by returning the feed to identity and seeding retention toward Cycle 002.

### Weekly pillar emphasis
- **Wk 10 — Countdown.** Drop beats escalate (T-minus framing, low-stock psychology). Full Cycle 001 lineup revealed on ATLAS (Uniform) with macro hero SKUs (Object). Standard sets the stakes ("why this exists").
- **Wk 11 — LAUNCH.** Drop day: Cycle 001 (10 SKUs) goes live via Shopify, email-led. Highest product density of the quarter, but a Standard Reel mid-week keeps it from feeling transactional.
- **Wk 12 — Sell-through.** Sustain urgency on the strongest SKUs (low-stock, last-chance). Object macros drive consideration; one Drop beat for the best-sellers.
- **Wk 13 — Re-anchor + tease 002.** Wind down selling. Return to The Standard, celebrate the men who bought in (identity, not transaction), and plant the first quiet seed for Cycle 002.

### Reel cadence
4 Reels / week through Wk 10–12 (peak cadence for launch energy), easing to 3 in Wk 13. All still The Work / The Standard — the Reels supply earned-not-hyped tension around the product posts.

### Key milestones / drop beats
- Wk 10: countdown opens; full lineup reveal.
- **Wk 11: Cycle 001 launch (10 SKUs live).** The pivotal beat of the quarter.
- Wk 12: sell-through / low-stock urgency on hero SKUs.
- Wk 13: sold-through messaging (if achieved) + Cycle 002 seed.

### KPIs to watch
- **Sell-through rate** (units / 10-SKU inventory — the headline metric).
- **Email → Shopify conversion rate** (primary channel performance).
- **Revenue + AOV** (premium positioning should hold AOV high).
- **Drop-day traffic spike** and **time-to-sell-out per SKU**.
- **Post-launch retention** (do buyers stay engaged for Cycle 002?).

## Quarter-level guardrails (apply to all three phases)
- **The Standard anchors every week** — even launch week. Identity never disappears under product.
- **Never two product days in a row** (The Uniform + The Object), in any phase.
- **Every Reel is The Work or The Standard** — product stays static.
- **Heavy product push every 7–10 days**, escalating in frequency toward the launch.
- **Pillar mix holds at ~30/25/20/15/10 across the quarter**; phase emphasis shifts week to week, not the overall balance.
- **Posting times:** 6:00 AM (intention), 3:00 PM (browse), 8:00 PM (cinematic / urgency) EST. See `posting-schedule.md`.
