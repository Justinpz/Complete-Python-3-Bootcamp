# ReRack — Caption Framework

> The written-voice system for the feed. Every line states a standard and lets the reader measure himself against it. Calm authority. A man with nothing to prove.

---

## 1. The Voice in One Paragraph

ReRack speaks like a disciplined man who has rebuilt himself and refuses to dramatize it. Short sentences. No filler. No hype. Intelligence implied, never performed. We do not sell motivation — we state a standard. The reader either rises to it or he doesn't. We acknowledge the cost of the work without wallowing in it. We assert; we never beg.

---

## 2. The Caption Formula

```
Line 1:  A standard or hard truth        (3–6 words)
Line 2:  Expansion, tension, personal truth
Line 3:  Brand stamp (FORGED DAILY.) OR a product-reference close
```

**Variants allowed:**
- **2-line minimal** — hard truth + stamp. Used for sharp anchors.
- **3-line standard** — the default.
- **4-line extended** — truth + two-beat expansion + stamp. Reserved for The Standard anchors that carry weight.

**Closes:**
- `FORGED DAILY.` — identity content (The Standard, The Work).
- **Product reference** — The Uniform, The Object, The Drop (e.g. "Heavyweight. Garment-dyed. Worn into the ground.").

---

## 3. Mechanics (non-negotiable)

- **Punctuation:** periods and the occasional em dash. No exclamation points. No ellipses for drama.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case in body; `FORGED DAILY.` and SKU labels (`CYCLE 001`) in caps.
- **Emojis:** none. Ever.
- **Hashtags:** optional, max 3, lowercase, own line, brand/relevant only.
- **Length:** as short as possible. Every word earns its place.

---

## 4. The Five Pillars (how voice shifts per pillar)

| Pillar | Voice lean | Typical close |
|---|---|---|
| **The Standard** (30%) | Philosophy, discipline, identity. The anchor. | `FORGED DAILY.` |
| **The Uniform** (25%) | The garment as worn identity. Quiet pride. | Product reference |
| **The Work** (20%) | Training, suffering, process. Raw, governed. | `FORGED DAILY.` or product |
| **The Object** (15%) | Craft, material, detail. Restrained reverence. | Product reference |
| **The Drop** (10%) | Scarcity, discipline of the cycle. Flat urgency. | Product / SKU reference |

## 5. The "Pull" Cross-Reference Mechanism

Captions can build a thread across days. One post **plants** a phrase; a later post **answers** it. This rewards the follower who reads every day and turns a week of posts into a small, earned narrative arc.

**The convention:** every caption may carry an optional pull-tag in italics under it:

```
*(pull: standard-held)*
```

- A pull-tag is a **lowercase, hyphenated theme key**.
- Captions that share a pull-tag are designed to chain. Authors scheduling the calendar place a **plant** earlier in the week and an **answer** later in the same or following week.
- The tag is an **authoring note only** — it never appears in the published caption. Strip it before posting.

**How chaining works in practice:**

> **Monday — plant** (`The Standard`)
> You fall to your standards.
>
> Not the day you feel strong. The floor you refuse to drop below.
>
> FORGED DAILY.
> *(pull: standard-held)*

> **Thursday — answer** (`The Work`)
> Today the standard held.
>
> No witnesses. No applause. The floor did not move.
>
> FORGED DAILY.
> *(pull: standard-held)*

The reader who saw Monday feels the payoff Thursday. The reader who didn't still gets a complete post. Nothing is lost; loyalty is rewarded.

**Common pull themes to chain:**
`standard-held` · `nobody-coming` · `rebuild` · `the-cost` · `discipline-vs-motivation` · `the-uniform` · `the-floor` · `quiet-work` · `cycle-001` · `earned-confidence` · `gone-when-gone`

In the caption packs, pull-tags are seeded so the calendar can wire plants and answers together. Use them or ignore them — every caption stands alone.

## 7. The Voice Test (run before any caption ships)

1. **Influencer check** — could a hype influencer have written this? If yes → rewrite.
2. **Contraband check** — any emoji, exclamation point, or cliché? → remove.
3. **Cut check** — is it shorter than it could be? → cut more.
4. **Standard check** — does it state a standard rather than beg for engagement? → required.
5. **Cringe check** — any "sigma," "stay hard," flags, eagles, "grind"? → kill it.
6. **Nothing-to-prove check** — does it sound like a man with nothing to prove? → ship.

If a line fails any check, it does not ship. Quality over cleverness. Never cheesy.
