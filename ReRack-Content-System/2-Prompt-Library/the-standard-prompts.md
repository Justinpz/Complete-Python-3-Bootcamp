# The Standard — Prompt Library

> Purpose: Text-focused philosophy scenes. Minimalist concrete and gym backdrops, a single shaft of hard light, and massive reserved negative space for caption/quote overlay. Mostly no model — or the model as a small, anchoring element. This is the identity backbone of the feed (30%).

---

### THE STANDARD — Single Shaft on Bare Concrete Wall   `STD-01`

**Higgsfield Prompt (copy-paste ready):**
A vast, bare poured-concrete wall fills the frame, steel-gray `#8A8D8F` aging into washed charcoal `#3A3A3C` in the shadowed corners. A single hard shaft of late-golden directional light cuts diagonally from the upper left, grazing the wall's micro-texture — form-tie holes, faint pour seams, hairline cracks — and falling off into deep crushed black on the right two-thirds of the frame. No model, no objects. The composition is built entirely around emptiness: the lit slice anchors the lower-left third while the upper-right two-thirds stay almost black, an intentional reserved text zone. Mood is monastic, severe, still — the gym as cathedral. Shot on a 50mm full-frame lens, slight wide-normal feel, deep depth of field so the wall texture stays crisp. Matte filmic grade, saturation pulled down ~15%, warm tungsten bias in the lit midtones, cool-neutral shadows, deepest blacks lifted 3–5% for a non-digital matte base. Fine consistent film grain across the entire frame. No lens flare, no light leaks, no haze gimmicks. Composed, unapproachable, quiet.

**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (feed)
**Use Case:** Anchor quote post — discipline / standard one-liner laid into the dark right zone.
**Caption Pairing:** "Discipline over motivation." / standard-not-motivation philosophy.
**Soul Character:** n/a — text scene

## Pairing Notes

Use these to keep a week of The Standard posts visually coherent without repeating a look:

- **Dark monochrome week (high contrast):** `STD-01` · `STD-02` · `STD-08` · `STD-15` — bare-concrete, single-shaft, deep-shadow consistency; rotate composition (wall / floor / corridor / graphic shadow) so no two feel alike.
- **Atmospheric / particle trio:** `STD-05` · `STD-17` · `STD-13` — chalk dust, cold breath, reaching hand. Strong "evidence of effort" sequence; space them across a week, not back-to-back.
- **Tonal-relief pair (warm/earth):** `STD-14` (bone) + `STD-18` (olive) + `STD-19` (tan) — drop one of these between dark posts to give the grid breathing room and pre-tease colorways.
- **Inverted-contrast accents:** `STD-12` (warm backlit) and `STD-21` (overhead fixture) read brighter — use one per ~9 posts to break the dark rhythm.
- **Product-whisper anchors:** `STD-07` and `STD-16` quietly seed product into Standard energy; good lead-ins to a Uniform or Object day the following post.
- **Vertical/story set:** `STD-03` · `STD-11` · `STD-21` are native 9:16 — batch these for a cohesive Stories sequence.
- **Cross-pillar bridges:** Any `STD` with an ATLAS small-element (`STD-04`, `STD-13`) pairs cleanly the day before a `UNI` or `WRK` hero shot to escalate from scale to subject.
