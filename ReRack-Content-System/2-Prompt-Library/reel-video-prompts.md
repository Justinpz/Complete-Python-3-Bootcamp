## Pairing Notes

Sequencing and reuse guidance for Reels (every Reel is Work or Standard energy; product never stars):

- **Hook strength:** lead a Reel set with motion that resolves fast — `REEL-01` (chalk clap), `REEL-06` (breath), `REEL-09` (hands macro) hook in the first 1–2s. Use these as the most-shared opener content.
- **Effort arcs (full payoff):** `REEL-02` (deadlift set-up→lockout), `REEL-07` (three-beat montage), `REEL-11` (carry) deliver a complete effort; ideal for music with a build/drop. `REEL-07` cuts on the beat — sync to percussion.
- **Meditative / text-over Standard set:** `REEL-03` (light crawl), `REEL-05` (corridor push), `REEL-08` (dust drift), `REEL-10` (stairwell ascent), `REEL-12` (gym wakes) — slower, philosophy-line-friendly; alternate these with high-energy Work Reels so the feed breathes.
- **Consistent close:** every Reel ends on a hard cut to black with the `FORGED DAILY.` stamp in warm-white `#F5F4F0` — keep the stamp identical across all Reels for brand cohesion. `UNI-18`, `WRK-20`, or `DRP-10` can serve as a static hero last-frame before the stamp.
- **Camera-grammar consistency:** smooth gimbal/dolly/crane moves only — no whip-pans, no shake, no frantic cuts except the deliberate on-beat hard cuts in `REEL-07`. Stillness and control over chaos, even in motion.
- **Cross-pillar pairing:** open a content week with a Standard Reel (`REEL-03`/`REEL-12`) to set tone, mid-week drop a Work effort Reel (`REEL-02`/`REEL-07`), and pair Reel themes with same-environment static posts the surrounding days (e.g. `REEL-06` ↔ `WRK-03`/`STD-17`, `REEL-10` ↔ `STD-03`, `REEL-01` ↔ `WRK-01`).
- **Launch use:** during a Drop, close a Work or Standard Reel on the `DRP-10` vertical launch frame instead of the plain stamp to convert energy into the Cycle 001 CTA.
