## Pairing Notes

Sequence Drop content as a launch arc across the cycle (Drops are only ~10% of feed — never bunch them, always punctuate with Standard/Work between):

- **Pre-launch / countdown phase:** `DRP-05` (countdown numerals, Stories) → `DRP-01` (countdown product + type) → `DRP-06` (mark + wordmark lockup). Build anticipation; space with Standard posts.
- **Launch-day phase:** `DRP-02` / `DRP-07` (model launch heroes) and `DRP-10` (vertical Story/Reel frame) — the "it's here" moment; `DRP-09` (reveal) and `DRP-03` (full lineup) confirm what dropped.
- **Scarcity / urgency phase:** `DRP-04` (single in spotlight) → `DRP-11` (low-stock stack) → `DRP-08` (sold-out empty hanger) as the closing beat.
- **Reserved-type cohesion:** every Drop frame leaves a dark or shadowed zone for "CYCLE 001" type — keep the wordmark placement consistent (upper or left zone) across `DRP-01`, `DRP-02`, `DRP-05`, `DRP-06`, `DRP-10` so the launch reads as one campaign.
- **Vertical set:** `DRP-05` and `DRP-10` are native 9:16 — batch for the Stories countdown + launch sequence.
- **Cross-pillar bridges:** lead a launch with a `STD` philosophy post (e.g. `STD-07` product-whisper or `STD-16`), follow `DRP` heroes with the matching colorway `UNI`/`OBJ` once live. `DRP-10` doubles as a Reel last-frame over any `WRK`/`STD` Reel.
- **Tone guardrail:** keep urgency cinematic and restrained — hard rim light and dark type zones, never neon, never hype-stickers, never exclamation marks. Drops signal discipline, not clout.
