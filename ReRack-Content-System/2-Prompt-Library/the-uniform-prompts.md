## Pairing Notes

Keep a week of Uniform posts cohesive while rotating colorway, pose, and light:

- **Colorway rotation (never two colorways back-to-back of the same hue):** Black (`UNI-01`, `UNI-05`, `UNI-08`, `UNI-11`, `UNI-13`, `UNI-16`, `UNI-18`, `UNI-19`, `UNI-21`) is the anchor; intersperse Bone (`UNI-03`, `UNI-09`, `UNI-14`), Tactical Tan (`UNI-04`, `UNI-10`, `UNI-20`), Washed Charcoal (`UNI-06`, `UNI-12`), Olive Drab (`UNI-07`, `UNI-15`), Cream (`UNI-17`).
- **Dark-void hero set (high cohesion):** `UNI-01` · `UNI-08` · `UNI-11` · `UNI-19` — charcoal-void chiaroscuro portraits; rotate pose (frontal / top-light / split / profile) so the look evolves while the world stays identical.
- **Warm-field tonal set:** `UNI-03` · `UNI-17` · `UNI-20` — bone/cream/tan on warm fields; use one between dark posts for grid breathing room.
- **Single-shaft light family:** `UNI-09` and `UNI-02` share the gym-shaft look — space them apart but they read as the same world.
- **Architecture / context heroes:** `UNI-04` (dock), `UNI-06` (corridor), `UNI-14` (stairwell), `UNI-15` (concrete wall) — strong environment storytelling; pair each with the matching `STD` environment the prior day (e.g. `STD-03` stairwell → `UNI-14`, `STD-08` corridor → `UNI-06`, `STD-10` dock → `UNI-04`).
- **Uniform → Object handoff:** `UNI-05`, `UNI-13`, `UNI-16` foreground the garment construction — schedule an `OBJ` macro the following day for a seamless product zoom-in.
- **Uniform → Work handoff:** `UNI-21` (chalk, focused intent) and `UNI-07` (seated recovery) bridge directly into a `WRK` training post next.
- **Vertical / Reel cover:** `UNI-18` is the native 9:16 hero — use as the cover/last frame of any Work or Standard Reel.
- **Pillar rule reminder:** never post Uniform two days in a row with Object — punctuate with a Standard between product days.
