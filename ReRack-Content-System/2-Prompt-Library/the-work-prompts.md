## Pairing Notes

Keep a week of Work posts varied across movement, light, and crop:

- **Lift-movement rotation (never repeat the same lift two Work posts running):** Deadlift (`WRK-02`, `WRK-20`), Press (`WRK-05`, `WRK-21`), Squat (`WRK-11`), Row/Pull (`WRK-09`, `WRK-15`), Carry (`WRK-18`), Conditioning (`WRK-17`).
- **Hands/macro detail set (bridges to Object):** `WRK-04` · `WRK-10` · `WRK-12` · `WRK-16` · `WRK-19` — chalk/grip/wrap/plate details; drop one between full-body lift heroes to vary the crop and rest the eye.
- **Atmospheric / no-model establishers:** `WRK-03` (breath), `WRK-08` (empty gym), `WRK-13` (loaded bar) — use to open a Work sequence or pair with a `STD` pre-dawn post the day before.
- **Chalk family (high engagement):** `WRK-01` (clap) · `WRK-10` (bowl) · `WRK-04`/`WRK-16` (chalked hands) — space across the week; never two chalk posts adjacent.
- **Backlit silhouette pair:** `WRK-07` (walk-out) and `WRK-15` (pull-up hang) share the backlit look — cohesive but space apart.
- **Recovery / realism beats:** `WRK-06` and `WRK-14` (sweat) — humanize the grind; alternate with high-tension lift heroes.
- **Cross-pillar handoffs:** `WRK-08`/`WRK-03` pair with `STD-02`/`STD-06`/`STD-17` (pre-dawn Standard); `WRK-04`/`WRK-16` hand off to `OBJ` fabric/embroidery macros; `WRK-20` is the native 9:16 Reel cover.
- **Pillar rule:** every Reel should carry Work or Standard energy — `WRK-20` is the anchor vertical, and `WRK-01`/`WRK-02` make strong Reel beat sources.
